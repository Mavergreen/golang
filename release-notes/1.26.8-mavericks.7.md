The toolchain now lives in `/usr/local/mavergreen/go126`, and the cross toolchain in
`/usr/local/mavergreen/go126-cross`. `go`, `gofmt`, `go-126` and `gofmt-126` are on the path of every
new login shell through `/etc/paths.d/mavergreen`, so nothing needs adding to `PATH` any more. The
cross toolchain is `go-126-cross` and `gofmt-126-cross`; on a box with both, bare `go` belongs to
whichever was installed first, and `sudo mavergreen select go go126-cross` moves it.

Programs built with an earlier release look for the bundled CA certificates at
`/usr/local/go126/etc/openssl`. Once that directory is gone they verify TLS against the system
keychain alone. Rebuild them to use the bundle at its new home.
